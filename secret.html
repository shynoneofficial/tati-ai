🔒 Secret Room — How it works

What is it? A private place where you talk to your people. Nobody else can see it. It is hidden.

1. Get a code: Tap "Get Code" and choose your secret code, e.g. 2580. Keep it secret.

2. Drag the green dot: When the lock box opens, you will see a small green dot. Hold it and drag it to your secret spot — anywhere on the screen. That spot is yours. It will stay there.

3. Tap 5 times: Next time, go to your secret spot and tap it 5 times. The Secret Room will open. Nobody will know where it is.

4. Enter code: Type your code to unlock. If you forget, tap "Get Code" again.

⚠️ Do not share your code. Do not share your secret spot. If someone knows both, they can open your room.
